## 2024-05-15 - Hoisting Invariants out of useFrame Loops | **Learning:** In high-frequency render loops like React-Three-Fiber's `useFrame`, re-calculating constant physics factors (like Re or rho0 based wake strength) inside a per-particle loop causes massive redundant CPU overhead. | **Action:** Always identify variables that are constant per-frame and hoist their calculation OUTSIDE the inner `for` loops before iterating over particles, saving hundreds of thousands of operations.
