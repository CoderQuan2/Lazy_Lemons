## 2024-03-27 - Optimizing React-Three-Fiber High-Frequency Loops | **Learning:** In high-frequency React-Three-Fiber `useFrame` loops (like calculating a 50x50 grid physics simulation running 2500 times per frame), redundant internal computations (like invariant math or O(N^2) division) can severely degrade frame rates. Pre-calculating invariants and converting division into multiplication (`1 / x`) provides measurable optimization. | **Action:** Always hoist variables invariant across loops out of the loop body and pre-calculate constants. Convert costly divisions to multiplications using inverse numbers whenever possible inside high-frequency `useFrame` physics loops.
